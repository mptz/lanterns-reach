# Ambiguities

Questions about the world and its lore that are deliberately left
unanswered, to keep a sense of mystery (`core/low-fantasy`: mystery is left
unexplained where explaining it would shrink the world or make it about the
player). These are not open design questions, which belong in
`design/open-questions.md`: an ambiguity is a decision not to answer.

Each entry states the question, the evidence the world may show (which
should support more than one reading), and what must never be stated
outright, so that content added later keeps the ambiguity intact.

## ambiguity/ruin-builders — Whether the animal peoples built Terra's ruins

- **The question:** who built the ruins that `antiquity` places across
  Terra (`worldgen/regional-fields`).
- **Evidence both ways:** there are no other obvious builders, but the
  ruins show a higher level of technology and civilization than the
  animal peoples now have (`peoples/peoples`). Tribes live in the ruins
  opportunistically, as later occupants would, or as descendants might.
  The peoples arrive from elsewhere (`peoples/sojourners`), which tips
  against them; the counterweight is visual: the living peoples' styles
  share motifs with the ruins, so it is never clear whether they made the
  originals or are copying them.
- **Never stated:** whether the peoples are the builders, their
  descendants, or strangers to them. No people claims the ruins as
  certainly its own or certainly not.

## ambiguity/enigma-makers — Who made Enigma, and why

- **The question:** who built Enigma, the archive and cabinet of
  curiosities, and to what end (`dimensions/enigma`).
- **Evidence:** players can learn a great deal about Enigma: its
  catalogues, specimens and devices. Its artifacts follow fixed hidden
  rules, so patient experiment learns *what* each does, never *why*
  (`worldgen/dimension-cosmology`).
- **Never stated:** no direct information about the makers, and no grand
  revelation of who they were or why they built it.
