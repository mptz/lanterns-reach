# flora/trees (draft) — A large catalogue of real trees, so no one feels obliged to collect them all

The world has a great many kinds of tree, around a thousand or more,
drawn from real trees. The aim is **abundance as set dressing**: with this many woods,
fruits and barks, no player feels they must collect every wood or visit
every habitat, in the way Skyrim's realistic clutter discourages picking
up everything that isn't nailed down. Trees are generated by
`worldgen/tree-generation` (`design/worldgen-features.md`); this file chooses
*which* trees exist and records what each is like. The candidate lists
live in `design/trees/regions/`, one file per region; full records
live in `design/trees/catalog/`, one file per slice of a region.

## Selection criteria
A tree is included if it meets at least one of these:
- **well known**, within its own region, not only worldwide;
- **in folklore or myth**;
- **culturally significant**, in any culture: national, state and
  provincial trees, sacred and ceremonial trees, and trees central to a
  people's crafts, foods or livelihood, not only those known worldwide;
- **striking or unusual:** colour, grain, figure, growth habit, form,
  flowers, longevity or size (ebony, purpleheart, balsa, bristlecone pine,
  dragon's blood tree);
- **conspicuous flowers;**
- **edible or interesting fruit, nuts or seeds;**
- **medicinal or toxic;**
- **product:** a notable product other than timber and ordinary food:
  resin, gum, latex, oil, fibre, bark, dye, tannin, spice, stimulant, wax
  or starch (frankincense, rubber, cork, cinnamon, cacao, urushi). Spices
  and stimulants count although they are eaten. Every tree that has one
  records it, whatever else it was included for, since products feed
  `crafting/material-classes`;
- **prehistoric:** documented extinct trees, and organisms that filled a
  tree-like ecological niche (prototaxites, lycopsid "scale trees",
  *Archaeopteris*). A subcategory.

**Grouping (what counts as one entry).** The unit is a tree a player can
tell apart, not a botanical rank. Relatives get separate entries when a
player could tell them apart in the world (form, bark, foliage, flowers,
fruit, timber colour or grain) or when they differ in habitat, products
or lore: Scots, ponderosa, lodgepole and Monterey pine; lychee and
longan; red and white oak; sugar and Japanese maple. Near relatives that
look, grow and are used alike stay one entry, which notes the relatives
it stands for, so no forest shows 25 interchangeable oaks. A large genus
may have many entries when each is distinct; what matters is that a
single place shows a believable handful, not how many the catalogue
holds.

**What counts as a tree** is cast broadly: a woody or tree-like plant
much taller than a person, including tree-like monocots, cacti,
succulents, bamboos and large shrubs that grow into small trees. No fixed
minimum height.

**Cultivars, clones and regional names.** One organism is one entry:
clones, cultivars and regional names for the same tree are collapsed into
it (its names list them). A cultivar or form gets its own entry only when
it is visibly different, such as a weeping or columnar habit or a
striking mutation. Notable individual trees (the Abarkuh cypress, General
Sherman) are lore on their species' entry, not entries.

**Extinct trees.** Trees extinct in historic times, or extinct in the wild,
are included as modern trees in their region, with the extinction noted
(`status`). The prehistoric subcategory is for the fossil record.

**Strata (for worldgen).** Each entry records the vertical layers it
occupies at maturity in its usual habitats, so worldgen can compose a
forest layer by layer. Strata are defined by position relative to the
local vegetation, by height and position only, never by woodiness (which
`form.habit` records), and never by fixed heights, since a canopy may be
10 m in one forest and 40 m in another:
- **emergent:** isolated crowns standing above the main canopy;
- **canopy:** the main roof of mature crowns, intercepting most sun and
  rain;
- **understory:** small trees living permanently beneath the canopy;
- **shrub:** low, usually multi-stemmed plants below the understory;
- **herbaceous:** the ground layer of low plants, and **floor**, the
  mosses, lichens, litter and fungi beneath them. Both are shared flora
  vocabulary, which trees rarely use;
- **open:** scattered trees in open country with no canopy above them
  (savanna, desert, parkland, treeline, mangrove fringe).

A tree may occupy several (`[canopy, emergent]`). Juvenile canopy trees
pass through the understory and are not listed there; **shade tolerance**
(`tolerant`, `intermediate`, `intolerant`) records whether a tree can grow
up beneath others, which drives understory placement and regrowth after
clearing: an intolerant pioneer such as birch fills gaps, a tolerant tree
such as beech or yew fills the understory.

**Tiers, for culling.** Each entry carries a tier: 1, core (iconic, or
strong on several criteria); 2, good (clearly meets one criterion and is
distinct); 3, marginal (meets a criterion narrowly, or is distinct mainly
to specialists). The target is 1,000–1,500 entries in all.

## Record schema
One YAML record per entry, in `design/trees/catalog/`. Units are SI;
unknown values are `null`, never guessed.

```yaml
- id: sugar-maple                 # kebab-case, stable
  names: [sugar maple, rock maple]
  scientific: Acer saccharum
  rank: species                   # species | genus | group
  stands_for: []                  # near relatives this entry absorbs
  distinct_from: [japanese-maple] # relatives with separate entries, and why
  tier: 1                         # 1 core | 2 good | 3 marginal
  status: extant                  # extant | extinct-in-wild | extinct, with the year in why
  inclusion: [well-known, striking, product]   # see Selection criteria
  habitat:
    range: eastern North America
    ecosystem: temperate deciduous forest
    climate:                      # bands, ordered; a range lists every band it spans
      temperature: [cool-temperate]   # cold | cool-temperate | warm-temperate | subtropical | tropical
      moisture: [humid]               # arid | semi-arid | seasonally-dry | subhumid | humid | wet
      note: null                      # the source's own wording, when it says more
    elevation_m: [0, 1600]
    substrate: well-drained loam; tolerates limestone
    niche: upland mixed forest
    strata: [canopy]              # emergent | canopy | understory | shrub | herbaceous | floor | open
    shade_tolerance: tolerant     # tolerant | intermediate | intolerant
    period: null                  # geological period, for prehistoric entries
  form:
    habit: single trunk, rounded crown
    height_m: [20, 35]
    trunk_diameter_m: [0.6, 1.0]
    lifespan_y: 300
    growth_rate: slow             # slow | moderate | fast; detail, if any, in growth_note
    leaves: deciduous broadleaf, palmately lobed
    seasonal: brilliant orange and red in autumn
    flowers: inconspicuous yellow-green, early spring
    fruit_visible: paired samaras
    bark: grey, furrowed into irregular plates with age
  timber:
    heartwood: light reddish brown
    sapwood: nearly white
    grain: straight; figured forms (curly, bird's-eye)
    texture: fine, even
    density_kg_m3: 705            # air-dry; game weights map from this later
    janka_n: 6450
    workability: hard to work by hand; turns and finishes well
    rot_resistance: not durable
    scent: null
    fuel: hot, steady, little smoke
    uses: [flooring, furniture, bowling pins, tool handles]
  products:
    food: [maple syrup from sap]
    sap_resin_latex: [sweet sap]
    bark: []
    leaves: []
    dyes: []
    medicinal: []
    toxicity: null                # what is toxic, to whom, how
  lore:
    folklore: []
    cultures: []
    symbolism: [national symbol of Canada]
    notable_trees: []
  game_fit:                       # proposals only; never research claims
    fields: {}                    # e.g. {liminality: high} with a reason
    dimensions: []                # terra | aether | enigma | ...; reasons go in hooks
    hooks: []                     # crafting, progression, first-session
  sources:
    - "https://…"
```

## Game-fit notes (proposals)
Speculative, clearly separated from the research: how a tree might sit on
the worldgen fields beyond climate (`alignment`, `liminality`, `weirdness`,
`savagery`, `design/regions.md`), which dimensions it suits
(`dimensions/terra`, `dimensions/aether`, `dimensions/enigma`), and
crafting or progression hooks (bow staves, charcoal, resins and dyes,
`crafting/material-classes`). Each note gives its reason, drawn from the
tree's real properties or lore (yew: long-lived graveyard tree,
toxic, so high `liminality`).
